Classic lemon bars

Prep 20 min, bake 45 min, makes 16 bars.

A buttery shortbread crust topped with a tangy lemon curd, finished with a dusting of powdered sugar.

Ingredients

2 cups flour, 1 cup butter, 4 eggs, 1.5 cups sugar, the juice of 4 lemons.

Method

Press the crust into the pan and bake until golden. Whisk the filling, pour it over and bake until set. Cool before slicing.
